## Study Protocol

1. Watch the relevant **Fall 2022** lecture video.
2. Read the corresponding **Spring 2026** slides.
3. Attempt the matching **Spring 2026 Discussion** before consulting worked solutions.
4. Solve **Spring 2026 Homework** independently before reading released solutions.
5. Follow the **Spring 2026 ASIC Lab** specification and available skeleton code.
6. Record commands, results, limitations, and remaining questions.
7. Complete the **Spring 2026 ASIC Final Project** or a documented adaptation.

**Environment note:** Public course instructions and RTL skeletons do not guarantee that proprietary EDA software, licenses, teaching servers or every required technology file are available outside Berkeley. Use an open-source flow where practical and document deviations.
